International Holocaust Remembrance Day

Observed on January 27 each year

Honours the six million Jewish victims of the Holocaust and all victims of Nazism.

Follow @days_pedia ↗Browse Daypedia

Why is International Holocaust Remembrance Day observed?

Honours the six million Jewish victims of the Holocaust and all victims of Nazism.

Who observes International Holocaust Remembrance Day?

United Nations; marks the 1945 liberation of Auschwitz.

Did you know?

Auschwitz was liberated on 27 January 1945.
